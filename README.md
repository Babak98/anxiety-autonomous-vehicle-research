# Anxiety & Autonomous Vehicle Research

Research project accompanying my article and thesis, bringing together anxiety-estimation and autonomous-vehicle notebooks.

## Research materials

The original notebooks and saved model artifacts are organized in the [code and model folder on Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1_v-p5tVLk0cbEalfpxKutMWeqJ7QUmMN). Access depends on the folder’s existing sharing settings.

| Material | Original file |
| --- | --- |
| Anxiety estimation notebook | `random forest anxiety estimator.ipynb` |
| Autonomous-vehicle notebook: anxiety | `anxiety_autonomous vehicle.ipynb` |
| Autonomous-vehicle notebook: non-anxiety | `non anxiety_ autonomous vehicle.ipynb` |
| Saved model artifact | `random_farest_model.joblib` |
| Saved scaler artifact | `random_farest_scaler.joblib` |

Original filenames are preserved here so the materials can be located easily. The roles above reflect their filenames; the saved artifacts have not yet been inspected.

## Article and thesis

This project accompanies my research article and thesis. The full article title, author list, publication link, and citation will be added after checking the final manuscript.

## Repository status

This repository currently serves as the public project overview and materials index. Notebooks and model binaries remain on Google Drive and have not been copied into this repository. Dataset files and defense documents are not published here. No benchmark scores or reproducibility claims are made at this stage.

## معرفی فارسی

این صفحه برای معرفی پروژهٔ پژوهشی مرتبط با مقاله و پایان‌نامهٔ من ایجاد شده است. فایل‌های پروژه شامل نوت‌بوک تخمین اضطراب، دو نوت‌بوک مرتبط با خودروی خودران، مدل ذخیره‌شده و فایل scaler هستند. از لینک بالا می‌توانید به پوشهٔ کدها و مدل در گوگل‌درایو مراجعه کنید؛ دسترسی تابع تنظیمات فعلی همان پوشه است.

مشخصات دقیق مقاله و نتایج پس از بررسی نسخهٔ نهایی مقاله به این صفحه افزوده می‌شوند.
